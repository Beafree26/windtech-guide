Wind Tech Starter Checklist

From Wind Tech Guide, written by working wind turbine technicians

1. Training and certificates

  • Created a free WINDA profile and saved my WINDA ID (winda.globalwindsafety.org)
  • Booked GWO BST at a GWO-certified centre: First Aid, Manual Handling, Fire Awareness, Working at Heights (+ Sea Survival for offshore)
  • Considered GWO BTT (mechanical, electrical, hydraulic)
  • Noted expiry dates: GWO certificates are valid for 2 years
  • Checked whether I need a medical certificate for the course or employer

2. Documents

  • Valid passport or EU ID
  • Driving licence (category B helps a lot)
  • Trade or electrical qualifications, translated into English
  • EU health insurance card (EHIC)
  • For posted work in another EU country: ask the employer about the A1 certificate

3. Gear (check what the employer provides)

  • Safety boots (S3)
  • Work clothes for cold and wet weather
  • Head torch, gloves
  • Never use your own fall-protection kit unless the employer approves it

4. CV and job search

  • 1–2 page PDF CV with certificates and WINDA ID at the top
  • Hands-on technical experience listed with concrete tasks
  • Languages and readiness to travel or work rotations stated
  • Applied to manufacturers, service companies and staffing agencies
  • Set up LinkedIn with "Wind Turbine Technician | GWO BST"

5. Before you sign

  • Know if it's employment or a B2B/self-employed contract, and who pays tax and social insurance
  • Hourly rate, overtime, travel time, accommodation and travel allowances written in the contract
  • Rotation schedule and notice period are clear

Independent guide. Not a GWO provider and not affiliated with GWO, WINDA or any employer. No job guarantee. Check requirements with your training centre and employer.
